ADSR editor
D 400 ms · S 62 %
A moves horizontally. D moves both ways and also sets sustain. S moves vertically, R horizontally. The time axis is cube-root, so 1 ms and 10 s are both easy to grab. The white node is the one being dragged.
Frequency display · grid, labels, analyzer, nodes, piano strip Log scale, 20 Hz–20 kHz. Labelled lines at 50, 100, 200, 500 Hz and so on up to 10k; unlabelled lines in between. The pre analyzer is a solid fill; the post analyzer is a translucent blue fill with a line on top. Nodes are 20 px circles labelled with the band id, and a dormant band is a hollow ring in its colour. On the piano strip, the hovered key lights up in the band colour. The same component draws the Poly Synth and Sampler filters, the Reverb low cut and the Delay feedback filter.
