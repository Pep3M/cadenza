Knob · sizes
S for compact views and secondary parameters. M and L for main parameters. XL for the one control that defines the device (Drive, Input gain). 270° sweep, arc stroke 2.5 / 3 / 4 px.
Knob · polarity, modulation, automation
Bipolar arcs start at 12 o'clock. The automation ring is violet (#c884e8): its dot shows the value now playing, the pointer shows the written value, and the readout turns violet.
Knob · states and floating value
Drag vertically: 200 px covers the full range, Shift ×0.1. The tooltip sits 2 px above the knob and never under the pointer.

An on toggle gets a tinted fill and a 1 px ring in its function colour: teal for stereo, amber for phase and warnings. Segments are for 5 choices or fewer; above that, use a grid (delay note values) or a dropdown. The amber dot means the preset has unsaved changes. A/B keeps two full states, and A→B copies one onto the other.

ADSR editor D 400 ms · S 62 %
A moves horizontally. D moves both ways and also sets sustain. S moves vertically, R horizontally. The time axis is cube-root, so 1 ms and 10 s are both easy to grab. The white node is the one being dragged.
Frequency display · grid, labels, analyzer, nodes, piano strip
Log scale, 20 Hz–20 kHz. Labelled lines at 50, 100, 200, 500 Hz and so on up to 10k; unlabelled lines in between. The pre analyzer is a solid fill; the post analyzer is a translucent blue fill with a line on top. Nodes are 20 px circles labelled with the band id, and a dormant band is a hollow ring in its colour. On the piano strip, the hovered key lights up in the band colour. The same component draws the Poly Synth and Sampler filters, the Reverb low cut and the Delay feedback filter.
