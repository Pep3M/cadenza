CADENZA · DEVICES 18 · Interaction spec, responsive rules, ideas
Shared rules (every control, every device) Hover The readout brightens and the floating value appears after 300 ms. In a display, a hairline shows frequency or time at the pointer.
Drag Knobs move vertically, 200 px for the full range. Nodes move on their axes. Shift = ×0.1 fine. ⌥ during a drag locks it to the axis it started on.
Wheel Over a knob: one step (1/100 of the range, 1/1000 with Shift). Over a node: its third dimension (Q, slope, width).
Double-click On a control or node: reset to default. On empty display space: create or wake the nearest band, zone or point.
Click the readout Type a value with units or shorthand. Tab moves to the next parameter in the device.
Right-click Automate (creates a lane and selects it in the arrange), Reset, Copy value, Paste value, Assign to MIDI CC, and Ask AI about this.
Automation Violet ring with a playing-value dot. Touching the control during playback switches it to Latch, and the ring turns solid until you release.
Keyboard With a device focused: ←→ select band, pad or zone; ↑↓ nudge the focused value; B toggles bypass; A/B switches compare; ⌘Z undoes with a named step (“Peak 2 gain”).
Animation Values ease over 80 ms when changed by preset, A/B, undo or the AI, and never while you drag. Analyzers fall at 12 dB/s. Peak holds last 1.5 s.
AI change Changed params get a blue ring and a delta tag, plus a summary card with Keep, Undo and hold-to-hear-before. Touching a changed param keeps that change and clears its ring.

Responsive: what collapses first (dock 1920 → compact 360) ≥ 1600 Everything shown. Knob rows at M/L, secondary displays (transfer, space, scope, harmonics) beside the main display.
1600 1. The header loses the undo hint and the A→B label. The Analyzer/Display row stays collapsed by default.
1400 2. Secondary displays shrink to 240 px; the knob grid goes from 3 columns to 2; large knobs shrink to M.
1100 3. Secondary displays move into a tab strip above the main display (Transfer · History on the compressor; Space · Tail on the reverb).
800 4. Header goes to two rows (the compact header). Knob row drops to S and wraps under the display; footer text is hidden.
560 5. Signal-flow instruments (Poly Synth, Sampler) become sections stacked vertically (OSC / FILTER / AMP) that scroll inside the dock.
360 6. Compact: main display at full width (EQ 344 × 196), selected-band or voice controls in one row of S knobs, everything else behind ▸.
The main display is never removed. Below 360 px the editor shows the device header and the main display only, with the panel's "Open in dock" button. The dock height can range from 320 to 420 px: displays take the extra height, and knob rows stay fixed.
Drawing budget (egui) Per display Under 400 shapes: about 1 analyzer polygon of 150 points, 1 sum curve of 240 points ×3 strokes for the glow, 7 band polygons of 120 points, 7 nodes and labels.
Data from the engine Spectrum: 1/6-octave smoothed bins, 150 points. History: one min/max pair per column. Scope: a 300-point trigger-aligned buffer. All at 30–60 fps.
Glow Two or three wide strokes at 5–20 % alpha under the 1.6 px line. No blur and no backdrop effects. Floating windows get a drop shadow mesh.
Idle state With the transport stopped, the analyzer decays to empty, history freezes and dims to 50 %, and the scope draws a flat line. Curves, nodes and envelopes stay at full strength.

Match EQ to reference Cadenza already analyzes reference tracks. Overlay the reference spectrum and propose a correction curve; Apply moves the 7 bands. Engine ready
Collision view In the EQ, show where another track (e.g. the kick under the bass) masks this one, as a red band on the analyzer. Engine
LFO and envelope modulation Drag a modulator onto any knob. The mod-range arc in the kit is already designed for this. DSP
Compressor sidechain filter An HP/BP filter on the key, shown as a small frequency strip under the history. DSP
Reverb freeze + ducking Hold the tail indefinitely, and duck the wet signal while the dry signal plays. Ducking draws as a GR trace on the tail. DSP
Delay per-tap editing Drag an individual tap to change its level or pan (a multi-tap delay). DSP
True-peak limiter oversampling A switch for 2×/4× oversampling, plus an ISP marker on the history. DSP
Sampler auto-map and round-robin Map dropped files from names like “C3_soft_rr2”, and draw a round-robin stack inside each zone. Engine
